Football Intelligence Engine

Research infrastructure before predictions.

The system is designed to make football analysis inspectable rather than opaque. Data quality failures are recorded explicitly, source evidence is preserved, and research outputs are intended to be reproducible and reviewable.

01 / DATA

Structured football data

Fixtures, team identities, schedule changes and market reference data are normalized into deterministic research inputs.

02 / EVIDENCE

Auditable evidence

Source snapshots and data-quality states are preserved so important analytical decisions can be traced back to their inputs.

03 / AI

AI-assisted analysis

Large language models are being evaluated for research synthesis, anomaly investigation, explanation and natural-language interaction.

Principles

Reliability is part of the product.

The project is being built with a controlled validation process: deterministic team mapping, explicit unmatched-fixture states, frozen evaluation universes, versioned schedule changes and isolated test accounting.

No silent failures

Unmatched or invalid observations are surfaced as explicit states instead of disappearing from the research sample.

Reproducible evaluation

Evaluation criteria are defined before a trial begins so results can be interpreted against pre-registered thresholds.

Human-reviewed identity

Provider-specific team identities are reviewed and approved to avoid runtime fuzzy matching in critical research workflows.

Current status
Controlled research & validation stage

Football Intelligence Engine is not presented as a finished consumer product. The current focus is validating data acquisition, identity resolution, evidence retention and research methodology before broader productization.
